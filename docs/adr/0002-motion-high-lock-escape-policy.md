# Treat motion high-lock escape as a shared v2 policy

Accepted. The v2 algorithm will model motion high-lock escape as a shared motion-stage spectrum-tracking policy, not as a TraceRescue-only patch or a final-curve postprocess. The policy targets the specific failure mode where a plausible lower heart-rate peak remains present in the motion-window candidate spectrum but the tracking state is pulled toward a higher motion artifact, harmonic, or protected wrong trajectory. Online triggering must not use reference heart rate, Lite comparison results, or any answer-derived signal; those are reserved for offline diagnosis and acceptance.

The first design uses a layered, explainable gate: a stable lower challenger, at least one high-lock risk signal, anti-regression guards, and multi-window confirmation before entering an escape state. Once confirmed, the solver may use a separate, more aggressive downward reacquire limit than ordinary physiological motion tracking, because the previous history is treated as an erroneous locked trajectory rather than a valid heart-rate trend. The mechanism is active only during motion adaptive spectrum tracking, updates the tracking history, may trigger more than once within a motion segment with a cooldown, and emits explicit state and reason fields for window-failure classification. Parameter selection should start with JSON-window-table replay over rescue, non-regression, and full-batch cohorts; it should not add Bayesian-optimization parameters until the mechanism is proven stable and interpretable.
